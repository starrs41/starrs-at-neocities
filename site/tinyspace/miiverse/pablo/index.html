Trivia

  • He wears shades constantly because his eyes are sensitive to light. He only feels comfortable removing them indoors.
  • He has dreams to travel and become a chef in a world-famous restaurant but is too indecisive and afraid to pursue them.
  • He had been living with family for a while, but decided to move out to gain more independence and to prove something to both his family and himself. After seeing an ad for the island, he made his way there.
  • He and Bloo are queerplatonic partners. Pablo originally fell for her first, but after talking things out, they decided that their current relationship was the best for them both.
  • He and Miko are romantic partners. They had a crush on each other but didn't act on it until later. (Thank Bloo for that.)
