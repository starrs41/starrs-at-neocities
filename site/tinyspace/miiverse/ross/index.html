Trivia

  • Ross loves playing sports. She constantly invites Bloo to do them with her, but notices that Bloo struggles to keep up with her. So, for Bloo's sake, she invites her out to jog too.
  • Something Ross wants to pick up is skateboarding. She often drags Max into because she heard that Max used to skateboard in the past. And also, she really just likes hanging out with Max.
  • Ross isn't afraid to tell a friend the truth, even if it may be hard to hear. (Although she does feel bad afterwards.)
  • She doesn't really understand Miis who would rather be alone or stay indoors. She finds it difficult to be in that position.
