Trivia

  • His home was pretty conservative, so he's happy now that he can experiment with more feminine clothing. He's still not confident in the clothes he picks out though, and will often ask for advice from others. (You can see how him and Miko get along quite well in this regard.)
  • Even though deep down he wanted a change, he was hesitant at first to take such a big leap and move to the island. After Akio said he'd be going, he found the courage to tag along.
  • He's soft-spoken except when he's angry. You don't want to see him when he's angry.
  • Him and Akio have been friends ever since they were kids. Even though circumstances have led them to talk less than before, their bond still remains as strong as ever.
